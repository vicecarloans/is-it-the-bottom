    How this works

    Every weekday after the US close, a job reads market data, checks five conditions, and counts how many hold. The conditions only count while the S&P 500 is at least 10% below its high of the past year.

    No
    0 to 2 conditions hold.
    Getting closer
    3 or 4 hold.
    Looks like a bottom
    All 5 hold.

    Two adjustments. If NVIDIA confirms in an SEC filing that Jensen Huang is its CEO again, the verdict moves up one level for 30 trading days. If credit markets break (high-yield bond spreads jump more than 3 points in 60 trading days) or the news reports a systemic failure such as a large bank collapse, the verdict can go no higher than "Getting closer".

    Not financial advice. This is a hobby project that tracks a rule of thumb. It can be wrong, and the rules have not been tested against history yet.

    Data: S&P 500 and sector funds from Yahoo Finance, rates and VIX from FRED, margin debt from FINRA, earnings from Finnhub.
